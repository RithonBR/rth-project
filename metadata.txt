about=60 integrated tools for remote sensing and precision agriculture. The RTH Scene Catalog connects QGIS to multiple STAC catalogs — INPE/BDC, Planetary Computer and AWS Element84 — to search, preview and download Sentinel-2, Landsat, CBERS and Amazônia-1 imagery directly within QGIS, compute vegetation indices (NDVI, EVI, SAVI, MSAVI, NDWI or a custom formula) reading only the selected field polygon via COG, and monitor deforestation in real time. GeoBase centralizes access to Brazilian public geographic data from FUNAI, ANA, ICMBio, IBAMA, INCRA, ANEEL and over 30 WMS/WFS sources in an integrated catalog, including elevation models (SRTM, Copernicus, AW3D) and PRODES deforestation data. For precision agriculture: planting and harvesting line generation with curve support, headlands, management plan export to 8 agricultural monitor brands with ISO-XML format for John Deere, and azimuth and line regularity analysis. For drones: mission planner with automatic GSD and overlap calculation, battery count and flight time estimation, export to Litchi, Mission Planner and KMZ, orthomosaic and DEM generation via GDAL. Four Google Earth Engine apps accessible directly from the menu cover temporal analysis, vegetation time series, climate data and deforestation monitoring. Automation tools — layer visibility cycling, area in hectares with automatic UTM projection, polygon reviewer, Google Earth Pro integration and File Geodatabase export — streamline the daily workflow for remote sensing professionals.
