about=The plugin was developed to support remote sensing professionals in their daily activities, bringing together 13 practical and integrated tools that streamline workflows within QGIS. Among its main features is a tool for visualization and download of imagery from multiple satellite sources, including Sentinel-2, Landsat, Amazônia, and CBERS, automatically identifying the most appropriate spatial reference system (EPSG) for the selected region. It also provides tools for quickly retrieving geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) directly from the map, As well as copying GeoJSON from drawn polygons to the clipboard. In addition, the plugin enables sending points to Google Earth Pro, facilitating integration between platforms. Another key feature is the ability to visualize and download Digital Elevation Models (DEM) for areas of interest. For spatial analysis, it includes a fast and accurate area calculation tool (in hectares) that uses the most suitable projection for the region. The plugin also allows importing GPS points and converting them into polygons, as well as generating evenly spaced georeferenced points within selected areas. Furthermore, it offers tools for quick CSV import and export, along with the creation of planting or harvesting lines within polygons, making it especially useful for agricultural and geospatial applications.
